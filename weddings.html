Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a wedding DJ cost in Leicester?

Evening entertainment starts from £545 and full day packages from £995. All prices are transparent — no hidden costs. A £100 deposit secures your date, with the balance due closer to the day. Get in touch for a personalised quote.

What does a wedding DJ package include?

All packages include a professional sound system, full LED lighting rig, setup and breakdown, the online music planning portal, first dance coordination, and DJ hosting. Full day packages also include ceremony music, wedding breakfast background music, cocktail hour, and full MC hosting throughout the day.

How long does a wedding DJ play for?

Evening sets typically run from around 7pm to midnight — approximately 5 hours of music. Full day packages cover the entire wedding day from guest arrival through to the last dance. Exact timings are agreed during the planning stage.

When should I book a wedding DJ?

As soon as you have your venue and date confirmed. Popular Saturday dates and summer weekends can book up 12–18 months in advance. Don't leave it too late — check availability now.

Can a wedding DJ also act as an MC?

Absolutely. The full day package includes complete MC and hosting services — guest announcements, coordinating with your venue team, managing the day's timeline, and keeping everything running smoothly so you can just enjoy your day.
