A Few Changes for 2028

Same service. Refined approach.

After more than 30 years behind the decks, I still love every event. Alongside TMW Productions I now work full-time in education, so from 2028 I'm refining how I work to protect the same high standard across the Midlands.

Everything you get on the night stays the same — a packed dance floor, a personal service, and fair pricing. It simply means the best dates go early.
