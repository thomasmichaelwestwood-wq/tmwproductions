About Faith

The person
behind the chair

Faith Peel is a Level 2 qualified hairdresser based at Journey 81 in Syston, Leicestershire. She specialises in precision cuts, creative colour, balayage and Trinity-certified hair extensions.

Her journey in hairdressing began at fifteen, training under experienced stylists in Leicester. Now fully qualified and building her clientele, Faith's approach is simple: listen first, then create something you'll love wearing every day.
