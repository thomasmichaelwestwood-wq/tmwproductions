About the Venue

Prestwold Hall, Leicestershire

Prestwold Hall is a stunning Grade I listed country house set in beautiful Leicestershire countryside. With its sweeping grounds, grand interiors and dedicated event spaces, it provides the perfect backdrop for a truly memorable wedding.

I've worked at Prestwold Hall many times and understand the acoustics, the layout and exactly what works for both ceremony and evening entertainment. Whether you're using the main hall for your reception or taking advantage of the outdoor spaces, I'll make sure the sound and atmosphere is spot on.

With all day hosting, I can provide wireless battery-powered ceremony music anywhere on the grounds — no restrictions on where your ceremony takes place.
