About the Venue

The Old Cow Shed

The Old Cow Shed is a beautifully converted agricultural building that has become one of Leicestershire's most sought-after rustic wedding venues. Exposed beams, stone walls and a relaxed countryside atmosphere make it a truly special place to celebrate.

Rustic venues like this one have unique acoustic properties — and I know exactly how to work with them. Uplighting transforms the raw character of the space into something magical, and I pride myself on getting the sound right in every corner of the room.

For outdoor ceremonies in the surrounding grounds, my wireless battery-powered system means your vows can happen wherever feels right — no wires, no limitations.
