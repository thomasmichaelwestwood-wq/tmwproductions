Planning Beats is a dedicated planning portal used by professional DJs. When you book with me, you get full access — completely free. No logins to pay for, no extra steps. Just a clear, simple way to get your event exactly right.

Search

Find Any Song You Want

Browse from millions of tracks using the built-in search. Found something you love? Add it straight to your list. Found something you'd rather never hear? Mark it as a must-not-play. Simple as that.

Music

Your Playlist, Your Rules

Build your must-play list, flag songs you definitely don't want, and add any special tracks for key moments — first dance, walkout, cake cutting. Everything in one place, ready when you are.

Timeline

Map Out Your Whole Day

Set your key times — when guests arrive, when dinner starts, when the first dance is, when the evening ends. I'll use this to make sure the music fits perfectly around every moment of your day.

Details

Tell Me Everything I Need to Know

A short set of questions covers all the important details — your venue, your vibe, your guests, any special announcements. Answer them when it suits you and I'll have everything I need well before the day.

One Tap Away

Save It to Your Home Screen

Planning Beats works as a web app — which means you can add it to your phone's home screen just like any other app. No app store needed. Tap it, you're in. It's that easy.

On iPhone: open the portal in Safari, tap the share icon, and choose "Add to Home Screen".
On Android: open in Chrome, tap the three dots, and choose "Add to Home Screen".
