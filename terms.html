The terms on which TMW Productions provides DJ and event entertainment services.

Event Booking Terms and Conditions · Last updated July 2026

1. Agreement to Terms

By confirming a booking with TMW Productions, the client or venue acknowledges and agrees to the terms set out in this document. No physical signature is required. Acceptance of the booking constitutes full agreement to these terms and conditions.

2. Payment Terms

All invoices issued by TMW Productions must be settled within seven (7) days of the invoice date, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Failure to make payment within this period may result in cancellation of services and/or additional charges.

3. Cancellation Policy

  • More than 30 days prior to the event: any cancellation must be made in writing. Deposits or booking fees paid are non-refundable.
  • 30 days or fewer prior to the event: the full contracted fee will become immediately payable and non-refundable.
  • This applies to cancellations or changes made by the venue, client, or any third party.
  • At the discretion of TMW Productions, payments made may instead be transferred to a new agreed date, subject to availability.

4. Behaviour and Conduct

TMW Productions expects all clients, guests, and venue staff to treat the DJ and any accompanying personnel with respect at all times.

The venue is responsible for ensuring that guest behaviour remains appropriate throughout the event.

Any form of verbal abuse, aggression, intimidation, or physical interference towards the DJ, equipment, or staff will not be tolerated. If such behaviour occurs, TMW Productions reserves the right to immediately terminate performance and withdraw from the event without refund.

Any damage to equipment or property caused by guests or venue negligence will be chargeable to the venue or client.

5. DJ Conduct and Professional Standards

TMW Productions is committed to maintaining a professional, courteous, and reliable service at all times.

The DJ will conduct themselves appropriately, dress suitably for the event, and operate within the venue's health and safety requirements.

During the warmer summer months, and in view of the rising temperatures now common in the UK, DJs working on behalf of TMW Productions may wear smart and suitable summer attire, such as tailored shorts and open collared shirts. Presentation will remain neat and professional at all times.

All performances will be delivered to a high professional standard, and the DJ will liaise courteously with venue staff, other suppliers, and guests throughout the event. If any issues arise during the event, they will be handled respectfully and, where possible, discreetly.

TMW Productions holds public liability insurance, and all equipment is PAT tested. Documentation is available to venues on request.

6. Provision of DJs

Wherever possible, your event will be covered personally by owner Tom Westwood. TMW Productions also works closely with a small number of trusted DJs in the industry, and on occasion an alternative DJ may be provided. This may happen, for example, where Tom is already booked and a request comes in from one of his recommended venues. You will always be informed of this at the time of booking.

This arrangement also means that, should the unexpected occur, professional cover is available and your event will still go ahead.

7. Event Planning and Start Times

When planning your event using the online planning tool, you may be asked what time your guests are arriving. This information helps the DJ understand who is likely to be present during setup, and should not be taken as the start time for music. Performance start and finish times are those agreed at the time of booking.

8. Force Majeure

TMW Productions will not be held liable for non-performance due to circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of nature, government restrictions, illness, or equipment failure despite proper maintenance. In such cases, any payments made may be transferred to a new agreed date, subject to availability.

9. General

  • All bookings are subject to availability at the time of confirmation.
  • Any amendments to these terms must be agreed in writing by both parties.
  • TMW Productions reserves the right to withhold services if payment has not been received in accordance with these terms.
