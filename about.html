The Story

More Than Just a DJ

I've been doing this since before most people had a mobile phone. What started as a passion for music and a love of seeing people enjoy themselves has turned into a 25-year career that I'm genuinely proud of.

I'm based in Leicestershire, and I cover events across the East Midlands and beyond. From school discos when I was just starting out, through years of nightclub residencies, right through to the most important day of people's lives — I've done it all.

What drives me is simple: I want every single person at your event to have the best time possible. That means reading the room, knowing when to build the energy and when to take it down, and always, always delivering for you and your guests.

In 2024, I entered The Wedding Industry Awards for the very first time — and won. Wedding DJ of the Year, East Midlands. Voted entirely by real clients. That meant the world to me.
