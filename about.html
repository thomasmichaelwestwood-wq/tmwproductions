The Story

More Than Just a DJ

I've been doing this since before most people had a mobile phone. What started as a passion for music and a love of seeing people enjoy themselves has turned into a 25-year career that I'm genuinely proud of.

I'm based in Queniborough, Leicestershire, and I cover events across Leicester city, Loughborough, Hinckley, Melton Mowbray, Market Harborough, Nottingham and the wider East Midlands. From school discos to country castles, shopping centres to stately homes — if there's a dancefloor involved, I've probably played it.

What drives me is simple: I want every single person at your event to have the best time possible. That means reading the room, knowing when to build the energy and when to take it down, and always, always delivering for you and your guests.

In 2024, I entered The Wedding Industry Awards for the very first time — and won. Wedding DJ of the Year, East Midlands. Voted entirely by real clients. Then in May 2026, I won again — Best DJ in the Midlands at the IDO Wedding Awards. Two awards, both voted by real couples. That means everything.

What You Can Expect

Personal Service

When you book TMW Productions, you're booking a personal, quality-controlled service — from first enquiry through to the last song. On occasion I work with other DJs I know and trust. Never an agency, never a stranger. The standard stays the same either way.

It's All About the Music

I take the time to understand exactly what you want — your must-plays, your must-not-plays, the vibe you're going for. Then I make it happen and adapt on the night as needed.
